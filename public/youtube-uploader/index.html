SoSoValue SoDEX YouTube Uploader

An internal tool that publishes SoSoValue's daily macro-analysis videos from our Lark workspace to our own YouTube channel.

What it does

A member of our team posts a video in a private Lark group along with a title, description and tags. The tool retrieves the video, shows a summary for the person to check, and — only after they explicitly confirm — uploads it to the YouTube channel we own.

  1. A video is posted in a private Lark group
  2. An authorised team member replies with the title and description
  3. The tool shows a preview and waits for confirmation
  4. On approval, the video is uploaded to our channel

Who can use it

Nobody outside SoSoValue. It is not a product, not a service, and has no sign-up. Access is restricted to a specific Lark group and a named list of team members; requests from anyone else are refused.

Google account access

The tool requests one Google OAuth scope: youtube.upload.

That permits creating new videos and nothing else. It cannot read, edit or delete existing videos, read analytics or comments, or access subscriber data — and it never attempts to. We deliberately avoid the broader youtube scope because the tool does not need it.

Videos are downloaded temporarily, uploaded, then deleted. No Google user data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or model training. Full detail is in the privacy policy.
